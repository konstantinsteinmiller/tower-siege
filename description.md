# Tower Siege

## Short description

*(147 characters)*

> Stack a tower, bolt on turrets, and hold the gate. Every wave hits harder — rebuild between them or watch your walls come down block by block.

## Description

*(486 characters)*

> Build a tower out of the blocks you are dealt, then defend it.
>
> Between waves you get a hand of four shapes — walls, archers, cannons, mortars, tesla coils — and a short window to place them. Then the horde arrives: goblins, boar-beasts, burning hounds, bone knights and siege engines that out-range everything you own.
>
> Blocks that lose their support fall, and falling rubble crushes whatever is underneath. Spend wood, stone and gold, unlock the tech tree, and see how deep you can get.

## How to play

**Build, then hold.** Each wave has two halves: a short build phase, then the fight. You cannot build while the horde is on the field, so what you place is what you get.

1. **Take a shape from the tray.** You are dealt four at a time. Slot one is always a structure piece, slot two is always a weapon, the other two are free. A shape you do not want can be rerolled.
2. **Place it on the tower.** Blocks need support — anything that ends up disconnected from the Gate falls, takes damage on landing, and crushes enemies it lands on. The ground row is limited to four cells either side of the Gate; higher floors can spread as wide as you like.
3. **Watch what the wave brings.** Each enemy punishes a different lazy tower. Runners close before long-range guns can whittle them; slingers stand off and out-range melee; flyers ignore the ground entirely; bombers open a hole in a wall of cheap wood; siege engines park beyond every turret you own.
4. **Call the wave early for a bonus.** Unused build time converts to extra resources — up to +40%. Calling early is a real trade, not a skip button.
5. **Protect the Gate.** Losing blocks is survivable and expected. Losing the Gate ends the run.

### Resources

| | |
|---|---|
| **Wood** | The cheap, fast material. Walls, braces, archers. |
| **Stone** | Scarcer. Heavier structure and the bigger guns. |
| **Gold** | Dropped by kills. Pays for the advanced blocks — mortar, bombard, tesla, frost, repair, mint — on top of their wood and stone cost. |
| **Coins** | Banked between runs. Spent in the tech tree on permanent upgrades. |

### Tips

- **Weapons make bad walls.** A tower of nothing but turrets folds the moment anything reaches it.
- **Height is not safety.** Bombers cruise above the tower and drop ordnance straight down.
- **A clean wave makes the next one harder.** Two waves without losing a single block raises the difficulty; taking damage resets it.
- **Cavalry is the answer to standoff engines.** Nothing in the tower reaches a trebuchet.
- **The tech tree persists.** A run that ends badly still pays into the next one.

## Controls

Everything is playable with one hand on a phone, or with a mouse on desktop.

| Action | Touch | Mouse / keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Pick a shape | Tap a tray slot | Click a tray slot |
| Place it | Tap a highlighted cell | Click a highlighted cell |
| Preview a shape's stats | Long-press a tray slot | Hover a tray slot |
| Reroll a shape | Tap the reroll arrow on a slot | Click the reroll arrow |
| Inspect or sell a block | Tap a placed block | Click a placed block |
| Pan the battlefield | Drag | Drag |
| Zoom | Pinch | Scroll wheel |
| Recentre the camera | Tap the recentre button | Click the recentre button |
| Start the wave | Tap **Call Wave** | Click **Call Wave** |
| Double speed | Tap the **2×** button | Click the **2×** button |
| Summon cavalry | Tap the cavalry button | Click the cavalry button |

The camera recentres itself unless you have panned away — the recentre button only appears once you have.
